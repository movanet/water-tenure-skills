# Human review gates

AI agents can register sources, extract records, draft and check at volume. They cannot be the ones
who decide that a scope is right, that evidence is good enough, that a report can be read by others,
or that a location can be made public. Each of those is a gate held by a named person. Record every
gate decision (who, when, what was read, what was decided) in the provenance log.

| Gate | Held by | Closes | The reviewer reads | Pass means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 Scope | The person directing the work | Phase 0 | The scope sheet | Area, period, question, exclusions, outputs, data protection and AI use agreed |
| G1 Catalogue | A researcher who knows the area or the method | Phase 2 | The checker output; the spot-verification note; a further sample of records read against their sources (at least ten, chosen by the reviewer, including every contested and every uncorroborated record) | The reviewer found no record that misstates its source, or the ones found are corrected and logged |
| G2 Report | The person responsible for the report | Phase 4 | The build report; the cite-check summary; the whole main text | The report may be read by its intended audience, with the unverified list in Annex H as it stands |
| G3 Legal referral | The person responsible, with the legal stream | Phase 5 | Annex D and the records behind each question | The questions are well posed and go to the right people; no answer has crept into the field report |
| G4 Publication | The person responsible, with the communities concerned where sacred or customary sites are involved | Phase 6 | The publication copy | Nothing on the map can identify a person or expose a sensitive point |

## What the reviewer is looking for

- **Sources that do not say what the record says.** The most common failure: a figure from one source
  attributed to another, a quote moved to the wrong village, a digest cited as if it were the original.
- **Confidence higher than the origins allow.** Two documents from one round of fieldwork counted as two
  legs; an agency's description of its own work counted twice.
- **Legal conclusions in the report's own voice.**
- **People who could be identified**, by name, by role in a small place, or by a precise location.
- **AI-generated material used as evidence.**
- **Absences reported as findings without saying what was searched.**

## When a gate fails

Do not patch around it. Return to the phase that produced the problem, fix the records or the text,
re-run the checks, and come back to the same gate. Log the failure and the fix; they belong in the
verification note.

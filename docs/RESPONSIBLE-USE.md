# Responsible use

These skills let AI agents do much of the work of a water tenure assessment. That makes some rules more
important, not less. They apply whoever runs the skills.

## AI is never evidence

- Text written by an AI system, including the agent's own drafts and summaries, is never a source for
  a record. It may point to an original; the original is what is cited.
- The skills check form mechanically (every statement cited, every figure attributed, vocabularies
  closed) and sample substance (spot verification, cite-check). Neither replaces a person reading the
  records against their sources.
- A clean checker run means the catalogue is well formed. It does not mean it is true.

## People decide at the gates

A named person decides at each gate (scope, catalogue, report, legal referral, publication) and the
decision is logged. Agents prepare gates; they do not pass them. Nothing is sent, posted, uploaded or
published without that person's explicit approval. See
`skills/water-tenure-assessment/references/review-gates.md`.

## Do no harm to informants and communities

- **Consent.** Use field material only within the consent under which it was collected. Record
  consent limits in the source register (`verifiability: restricted`) and respect them in every output.
- **Anonymity.** Cite respondents by code and role, never by name. Keep the key from codes to people
  outside the catalogue, with the team. A role can identify a person in a small village; generalise
  the role when it would.
- **Allegations.** Bribery, theft, threats and similar claims are recorded as patterns, graded
  uncorroborated until corroborated, without naming individuals or offices that would identify them.
- **Locations.** Informal, assumed, impossible and unrecognised uses, sacred sites and the subjects of
  allegations are shown only approximately on any public map. Ask the community before publishing the
  location of a customary or sacred site at all.
- **Social media.** Private persons are never identified; their posts are clues, not evidence.
  Institutional accounts may be named.
- **Photos.** Only with the consent of the people shown; check for faces and embedded location data
  before publishing.

## The legal boundary

The field stream describes practice and asks legal questions; it does not answer them. A report that
calls a community's water use "illegal" in its own voice can do real harm to that community and is
outside what the evidence of a field assessment can show. Legal questions go, with their records, to
the people doing the legal assessment.

## Disclose AI use

Every deliverable says who directed the work and set the method, which AI agents and models drafted
which parts, what a person checked, and what no one has yet verified. Transcripts of agent sessions are
not published; directions are paraphrased. Where a review cannot be shown, the report says "not
evidenced", not "reviewed". The report skill's Annex H and the assessment skill's provenance guide
show how.

## Tools, sites and data

- Respect the terms of the sites and platforms you search. Do not get around logins, paywalls or
  CAPTCHAs; leave blocked sources for a person.
- Keep API keys, cookies and browser sessions in the tools' own configuration on the operator's
  machine. Never put them in a project folder, a catalogue, a commit or a report.
- Cached copies of web pages and documents used for verification may be under copyright. Keep them in
  the project's private working area, not in public repositories.
- Ask before downloading data (for example boundary or river layers); state the source, size and
  licence, and credit it on every map.
- Keep transcripts, catalogues of real places and reports out of public repositories unless their
  owners have decided to publish them.

## Limits of this software

The skills were written by AI coding agents under human orchestration and tested on synthetic
examples. They encode one method, built from FAO's water tenure work and CRPG's assessments; they are
not a standard. Classification calls are judgements that the skills structure but do not make for you.
Review the software, and the outputs, before relying on them.
